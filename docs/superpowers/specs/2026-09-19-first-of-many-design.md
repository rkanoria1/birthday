# First of Many — Experience Design

## Purpose

Somya's October 6, 2026 birthday is Rahul's first opportunity to celebrate her
while they are engaged and still getting to know one another. The site must make
her feel observed, celebrated, reassured, and excited about their future without
pretending they already share years of memories or married life.

## Emotional arc

1. **Anticipation:** a private birthday delivery addressed only to Somya.
2. **Celebration:** a glamorous portrait-led birthday premiere.
3. **Play:** a no-wrong-answer birthday mood choice.
4. **Recognition:** honest things Rahul has noticed so far.
5. **Admiration:** four qualities already visible to him.
6. **Agency:** Somya chooses a wish for the year ahead.
7. **Reassurance:** a hopeful note from “Rahul, one birthday from now.”
8. **Intimacy:** candle wish and sincere present-day letter.
9. **Real-world payoff:** “Now come find your Chandler.”

## New interactions

### Birthday mood

Somya chooses between a quiet evening, small adventure, or dressed-up
celebration. Every option is accepted. The response shows Rahul's warm guess and
reinforces that learning her preferences is part of the gift.

### Birthday time capsule

Somya selects one wish—peace, adventure, laughter, growth, or “surprise me.” The
site creates an attractive promise card containing her choice and the date. It
is local-only, screenshot-friendly, and requires no account or data submission.

### Future Rahul note

A sealed note dated October 6, 2027 opens to hopes, not fictional memories. It
looks forward to knowing her better and celebrating the first year of shared
milestones.

## Content voice

- Say “I am looking forward to,” not “we always.”
- Say “what I have begun to notice,” not “everything I know about you.”
- Express reliable, modest promises rather than sweeping certainty.
- Keep humor gentle and make the final Friends line the only fandom reference.

## Visual continuity

Retain the cinematic burgundy entrance, editorial photography, living ambient
background, glass progress/navigation, and responsive motion language. New
chapters receive distinct visual identities while using the existing palette,
type system, accessibility rules, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Success criteria

The complete flow feels like an attentive first celebration between two people
at the beginning of a relationship. Choices never test or judge Somya. Every
interaction works with touch and keyboard, the main flow remains usable without
photos or sound, and the experience passes tests, lint, production build, and
phone/desktop visual review.
